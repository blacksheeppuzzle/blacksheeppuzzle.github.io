KVKK Information Notice

Last updated: November 18, 2026

In short. KVKK is Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 6698). Black Sheep is published by a Turkish sole proprietor, so Turkish residents have the rights the law gives them. They are written out in the Turkish notice, which is the binding text.

Turkish residents

If you live in Türkiye, the KVKK Aydınlatma Metni (the Turkish information notice) explains who is responsible for your data, why and on what legal basis we process it, who receives it, how long we keep it, which rights you have under Article 11 of the law, how to apply, and how to complain to the Personal Data Protection Board. It also contains the consent wording and an application form.

The full Gizlilik Politikası ve KVKK Aydınlatma Metni lists every kind of data the game processes.

Everyone else

Our Privacy Policy (English) describes the same processing and the rights that apply to you wherever you live. For a request under U.S. state laws, use the Privacy Rights Request page.
