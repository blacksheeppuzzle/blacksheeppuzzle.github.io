Your progress, wool, hints, streak and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us. We never ask for an e-mail, real name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or birthday.

3. How data is collected

Electronically, automatically while you use the app, and through the leaderboard name you enter.

4. Transfers

To provide the service, data is transferred to: Supabase (servers in the EU, Sweden), Google (AdMob, Firebase; including the USA), RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) and Apple (payments and push). Transfers abroad rely on appropriate safeguards under Article 9 KVKK such as standard contracts and, for the optional purposes (analytics, personalised ads, notifications), also on your explicit consent. Data may be disclosed to authorised public bodies where the law requires.

5. Retention

Account, leaderboard name, results, reports and blocks: until you use "Delete my data". Analytics: 2 months. Crash reports: 90 days. Push token: until you turn notifications off. Purchase records: as long as needed for restoring purchases and by tax law (Apple and RevenueCat).

6. Your rights (Article 11 KVKK)

You may learn whether your data is processed and request information about it; learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad; request correction of incomplete or wrong data and its deletion or destruction under Article 7, and that these be notified to the third parties; object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy and KVKK.

7. How to apply

Write to blacksheeppuzzle@gmail.com and mention your leaderboard name so we can find your data. We answer free of charge within 30 days at the latest, under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. Settings → Delete my data in the app deletes all your server data at once. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.
