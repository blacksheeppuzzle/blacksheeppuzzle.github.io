1. Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy explains what information Black Sheep (the "Game") collects, why, who receives it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Black Sheep mobile app for iOS and Android and the pages linked to it. Where iOS and Android differ, we say so in the relevant section.

The Game has no accounts or login screens. It does not ask for your email address, phone number, real name or date of birth, so it collects very little. Everything we collect is listed below.

4. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Run the Game, save your progress, show the daily leaderboard, and display your nickname and avatar to other players.
  • Verify purchases and subscriptions, and grant the weekly wool included in Shepherd's Club.
  • Review reports, prevent cheating and abuse, and keep the service secure.
  • Understand crashes and improve levels, only if you allow analytics and crash reports.
  • Show ads, and show personalized ads only if you opt in.
  • Send the daily reminder and daily-winner notifications, only if you opt in.
  • Comply with law, respond to your requests, and protect our legal rights.

6. Your choices

Everything that needs permission is off by default, and you can change your choices at any time.

  • Analytics and crash reports: Off until you agree. They run only if you give consent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & KVKK.
  • Personalized ads: When off, all ads are non-personalized. When you turn it on, iOS asks for tracking permission; if you decline, your advertising ID is not used. You can change this choice in Settings > Privacy & KVKK, in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or, on Android, in your device's ad settings.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent form is shown, and you manage ad choices there. You can reopen it any time in Settings > Ad privacy options.
  • Notifications: Sent only if you tap "Remind me" (or turn on Settings > Notifications) and allow notifications on your device. You can turn them off in Settings > Notifications or in your phone's settings at any time.
  • Delete your data: Settings > Delete My Data (see Section 11).

8. Security

We send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), limit server access to authorized people, and keep access keys secret. Because we do not collect email addresses, phone numbers or real names, the risk to your identity from a breach is low. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children: 13+ and COPPA

Black Sheep is for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them, as the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requires. If you believe a child under 13 uses the Game, email blacksheeppuzzle@gmail.com and we will delete the related data.

We do not share the personal information of users under 16 for targeted advertising unless they opt in. Personalized Ads are off by default and require an opt-in in the Game and, on iOS, in the tracking prompt. Parents and guardians of teens should review the in-app purchase and permission settings.

11. How to exercise your rights

Delete in the app. Settings > Delete My Data permanently deletes your anonymous account and all linked data on our servers (profile, scores, reports, blocks, daily wins), plus the save data and consent choices on your device. It does not delete purchase records held by Apple or Google Play and by RevenueCat, which are needed to restore purchases and meet legal duties. Firebase data expires on Google's schedule (analytics 2 months, crash reports 90 days). The deletion cannot be undone, removes unspent wool and hints, and does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your store's subscription settings (iOS: Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions; Android: Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions).

Other requests. Email blacksheeppuzzle@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Request" and say which right you want to use. Because there are no accounts, include your nickname and the Support Code shown in Settings > Support & FAQ so we can find your record. We may ask for more details to verify it is you; if we cannot verify, we may not be able to act on the request.

Timing. We confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. We may extend this once by another 45 days and will tell you why.

Agents. You may use an authorized agent; we will need written proof of their authority.

Appeals. If we refuse a request, reply to our answer with the subject "Appeal" and we will respond within the period your state's law requires. If you are still unsatisfied, you may contact your state attorney general.

Privacy rights request. You can send a privacy request with the form on our Privacy Rights Request page.
