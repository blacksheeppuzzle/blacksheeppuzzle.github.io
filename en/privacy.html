Privacy Policy

Black Sheep: Logic Puzzle (iOS) · Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short. There is no sign-up. We never ask for your e-mail, real name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or birthday. Your game progress stays on your phone. Only the daily-puzzle leaderboard, purchases and ads use the internet. Analytics, crash reports, personalised ads and notifications are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off again at any time.

2. Data that stays on your phone

Your level progress, stars, wool, hints, daily streak, settings, consent choices and what you own from the shop are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the app or using "Delete my data" removes them.

4. Transfers outside your country

Supabase stores our game data in the EU. Google, RevenueCat and Apple may process data in the United States and other countries. These transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework; for transfers from Türkiye, on the safeguards and, for optional purposes, your explicit consent described in the KVKK Information Notice.

5. Your choices

  • Analytics, crash reports and personalised ads: Settings → Privacy and KVKK. Both start off.
  • Google's ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Tracking: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Notifications: Settings → Notifications in the game, or the iPhone's Settings.
  • Block a player on the leaderboard (long-press their name); unblock in Settings → Blocked players.

The game works fully whatever you choose. Nothing is given or taken away for allowing data use.

6. Delete my data

Settings → Delete my data deletes your anonymous account and everything linked to it on our server (profile, scores, reports, blocks), resets the analytics id, removes your push token, and wipes the game from your phone. It needs an internet connection; without one, nothing is deleted and the game tells you. Purchase records stay with Apple and RevenueCat so that you can restore your purchases; to ask RevenueCat to delete them, write to us. An active subscription is not cancelled by deleting data: cancel it in your Apple Account.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the GDPR in the EU/UK or KVKK in Türkiye) you may ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing before withdrawal). Because we do not know who you are, please tell us your public leaderboard name, or use the in-app settings, which act immediately. We answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

8. Children

Black Sheep is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Ads shown in the game match its age rating. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Our database allows each player to change only their own data. Service keys are kept out of the app's code and our repository.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
