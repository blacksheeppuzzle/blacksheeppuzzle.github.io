Black Sheep Support

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Questions, feedback or a problem? Email blacksheeppuzzle@gmail.com. We usually reply within 3 business days. For a technical issue, include your Support Code (Settings > Support & FAQ), device model, iOS or Android version, app version and, if possible, a screenshot.

Frequently asked questions

  • My purchase didn't arrive, or I'm on a new phone. Open the Shop and tap "Restore purchases."
  • How do I cancel my subscription? On iPhone go to Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions; on Android open Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • I want a refund. Purchases are made through Apple or Google Play, so request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com (iOS) or from your Google Play order history (Android).
  • I want to delete my data. Use Settings > Delete My Data. If you cannot open the app, email us.
  • How do I turn off personalized ads? Switch off Personalized Ads in Settings > Privacy & KVKK, or use iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking (iOS) or your device's ad settings (Android).
  • How do I turn off notifications? Settings > Notifications in the app, or your phone's notification settings.
  • I saw an inappropriate nickname. Report or block the player from the leaderboard; we review every report. You can manage blocks in Settings > Blocked players.
  • I want to make a privacy request. See our Privacy Rights Request page.
