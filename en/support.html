Support and FAQ

Black Sheep: Logic Puzzle (iOS)

Contact us: blacksheeppuzzle@gmail.com. Please mention your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (Settings, at the bottom). We usually answer within 2 working days.

Playing

What are the rules?

Fill the board with white and black sheep. (1) Every row and column has as many white as black sheep. (2) Never three sheep of the same colour next to each other in a row or column. (3) Two cells joined by a rope (=) hold the same colour. (4) Two cells split by a cloud (×) hold different colours. Fenced sheep are given and cannot be changed. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone.

How do I place sheep?

Tap an empty cell for a white sheep, double-tap for a black sheep, tap a sheep to remove it.

What are the hay bales?

From level 3 you have three hay bales. A move that breaks a rule costs one. With none left you can watch one optional video once per level for one more bale, or restart. Shepherd's Club's Comfort Mode removes hay bales.

How do hints work?

A hint first shows a wrong sheep if there is one; otherwise it highlights the row or column to look at, then the shepherd explains the step, then the cell is filled in. Hints are bought with wool.

What is the daily puzzle?

It opens after level 11. Everyone gets the same puzzle each day (it changes at your local midnight). Solving it gives wool and keeps your streak; your first solve of the day counts on the leaderboard.

Leaderboard and names

How do I change my name or avatar?

Tap your avatar on Home → edit. Names are 3–16 letters, digits or _, and must follow the rules in our Terms of Use.

How do I report or block a player?

Long-press the name on the leaderboard → Report name (and "Also block") or Block. We review reports within 24 hours. Unblock in Settings → Blocked players.

My name does not appear on the leaderboard.

The server may have refused it: choose another name. A name reported by several players is hidden until we review it.

Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Settings → Restore purchases (or the link at the bottom of the shop), signed in with the same Apple Account. Subscriptions and the Starter Pack come back. Wool and hints are kept on the device only and cannot be restored after deleting the app.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Black Sheep → Cancel, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. It stays active until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds; we cannot refund purchases ourselves. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but did not get my item.

Close and reopen the app with an internet connection, then try Settings → Restore purchases. If it is still missing, write to us with the date of the purchase (never send card details).

Privacy, ads and notifications

Do you collect my personal data?

We never ask for your e-mail, name or phone number. See the Privacy Policy. Analytics, crash reports and personalised ads are off until you turn them on in Settings → Privacy and KVKK.

How do I remove ads?

Shepherd's Club, Ad-Free or the Starter Pack (7 days) remove banners and interstitial ads. Reward videos are always optional.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Settings → Notifications. We send at most one reminder a day, in the evening.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete my data. It deletes your account and everything on our server, and resets the game on your phone. It needs an internet connection. Cancel subscriptions separately in your Apple Account.
